How It Thinks

Examiners have habits

Nobody writes a brand-new exam from scratch every year. The same favourites keep coming back — in patterns:
✅ The favourites
Some topics appear every single year. Until that changes, they're the safest bets in the paper.
📈 The growing ones
Some topics get bigger every year. That's an examiner leaning in — expect more next time.
🔄 The alternaters
Some topics take turns — on the paper one year, off the next. If one was skipped last year, it's due.
📉 The fading ones
Some topics have quietly disappeared from recent papers. Good news: less time on those.
And one simple rule underneath: recent exams matter more than old ones, because courses and examiners change.
