Signal Decoupling

Signal 1: Objectives vs. Signal 2: Theory

Treating all exam questions the same ruins accuracy. We split them:
Signal 1: Objective Questions
• Broad Coverage is Guaranteed: Presence/absence is noise.
• The True Signal is Density: How many questions per topic per year.
• A topic with 8 out of 50 questions every year is a Core Staple; a topic with 1 question is just random filler.
Measured by Question Density
Signal 2: Theory Questions
• Strict Slot Scarcity: Only 4 to 6 theory questions exist (20 marks each).
• The True Signal is Deliberate Selection: Which topics get awarded the massive 20-mark slots.
• A topic claiming a 20-mark slot in 3 of 5 years is a dominant curriculum anchor.

Time-Series Intelligence

Yearly Patterns: Beyond Raw Frequency

We build a yearly time-series per topic and classify its structural trajectory:
● Staple
Appears every single year with stable density. Predict with highest confidence (1.0x).
📈 Rising
Marks strictly climbing (3 → 5 → 9). Predict heavy upcoming coverage (>1.0x).
📉 Fading
Heavy in earlier years, disappearing recently. Deprioritized (<1.0x).
🔄 Alternating-Due (The Killer Insight)
Appears every other year (negative correlation with 2-year lag). If last year was skipped, it receives a huge boost (1.4x) because it is DUE NOW!
⏳ Alternating-Rest & Erratic
If just tested in an alternating cycle, it receives a penalty (0.4x). Erratic topics with no rhythm are flagged as unpredictable.
